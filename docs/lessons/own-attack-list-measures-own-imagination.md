A list of attacks written by the builders shows what the builders thought of, so "all our tampering checks are caught" is not evidence that the product is safe.

- **What happened (2026-11-17):** the evaluation had 29 tampering checks, all refused by the product, and the handoff said so. A reviewer with fresh context, told only to break the four success measures, found six more in a few hours; four of them turned a real covenant breach into a pass with nothing raised. One existing check ("a mis-mapped debt row can no longer hide a breach") passed only because it used the full row label; the reviewer used a fragment.
- **Why:** each check was written right after its fix, by the person who wrote the fix, using the input the fix was designed for.
- **Rule:** after any change to a guardrail, have someone who did not write it attack it, and vary every existing attack (fragment, synonym, neighbouring row, other page) before calling it closed. Report tampering results as "these N mistakes are refused", never as "mistakes are refused".
- **How to apply:** the brief for the reviewer names the promise to break, not the checks to run. Add every hole found to `evals/mutations.py` so it stays closed.
- **And again:** after those six were fixed, a second fresh reviewer broke the same promise in new ways within one session (page layouts, units, signs, a figure reported four times, date-shaped and list-shaped figures in prose). Fixing the reported cases one by one had left their causes in place. What held up better was removing the cause: stop trusting any reader-supplied locator, and refuse whatever is not recognised instead of listing what is dangerous. Expect a third reviewer to find something; budget for it.
- **A third time (2026-11-18):** a third fresh reviewer found seven more, the worst in the borrowing base reader, which had the same reader-supplied labels the statement reader had been cured of two rounds earlier. Lesson within the lesson: when a class of defect is found in one reader, fix it in every reader the same day; do not wait for a reviewer to find each one.
- **What to conclude:** passing your own checks, and even passing two independent reviews' worth of fixes, is not evidence that a reading is right. The product now defaults to a person confirming every document reading (`EXTRACTION_AUTONOMY=assisted`), and `automatic` has to be earned with accuracy measured on real documents. Say this to the owner in plain words; do not report "all checks pass" as "safe".
