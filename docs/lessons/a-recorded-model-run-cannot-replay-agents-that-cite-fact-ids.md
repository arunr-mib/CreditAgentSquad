A live record pass measures the model; a replay of it fails for any agent that cites fact ids, so the live scorecard, not the replay, is the evidence until fact ids are stable.

Confirmed 21 November 2026 (first full live record pass on claude-opus-5-5, 20 borrowers, 8.31 US dollars measured from the recorded `usage` fields against an estimate of 12.65 mid; the owner capped the pass at 21).

- **What the live pass showed (record mode, every cycle graded against the signed key).** Gate passed in every batch: no invented-number, no false-compliant, no run-error, nothing-read none. Extraction 1,621 of 1,621 facts; covenant outcomes 56 of 59 (the 3 not reached are Stripple's, held for a person); exception precision and recall 100%; 18 of 20 cycles closed (Lumora waits for its late package, Stripple rests on held figures, both as the key expects); cycles needing no person 55.0%, as the key expects. Named failures: wrong-abstain 2 (the Covenant Agent reached its 12-step cap for Dataflake and Kestrel Bay; code still raised both exceptions).
- **What the replay shows.** Replaying the same recordings (`--mode recorded`) fails the gate: 6 innovation cycles end in `RecordingMissing` at `review/02`, the Covenant Agent for Dataflake and Kestrel Bay hits its step cap, and the Review and Early Warning answers lose sections with `fact_unknown`. Cause: fact ids and document ids are random per run (`uuid4`), the recorded answers cite the ids of the recording run, so every cited fact is "not in scope" in the replay and the agent retries into an exchange that was never recorded. This is a harness and product limit, not a model result (known since run 2 on 18 November; it now blocks the recorded tests and CI replay for the two citing agents). A whole-book legacy replay also fails by design: only the kept three legacy borrowers have recordings.
- **What the live run found in the product (for the controller, not fixed here).** (1) The number guardrail drops honest spreading notes: "Note 1 on page 5", "page five", "(ending 9001)" are counted as typed figures; 41 notes dropped across 17 borrowers, and the dropped text is the test case (see `an-allowance-for-honest-text-is-a-hole`: narrow it with refusal tests for amounts that must still be refused). (2) The Covenant Agent counts tool calls, not exchanges, against a 12-step cap; Opus reads the pages in parallel batches and runs out (2 of 4 investigations). The same investigations in Corvane and Pellham were retried after `figure_untraceable` and `number_in_words` refusals ("Section 6.3", "three months", "four quarters") before they completed. (3) The Review Agent's `risks` section is dropped when it cites no fact (Ferrogate, Harrowgate). (4) Two evals tests still assume an unsigned key (`test_book_registry`, `test_signoff`) and fail now that all 107 rows are signed.

Rule: judge a live pass by its record-mode scorecards (one per batch under `evals/results/live-2026-11-21-*`), not by a replay, until fact ids are made stable per run (or recordings map the recorded ids onto the replay's); do not call a replay result a regression. Price from the recorded `usage` fields (input 4, cache write 5, cache read 0.2, output 20 US dollars per million), not from the estimate; the estimate ran 1.5 times too high here because spreading calls on monthly packs, MRR and bank statements are shorter than the three recorded statement spreads.

Applies to: `evals/run.py` recorded mode, `backend/app/agents/llm.py` (`RecordedClient`), the Review, Early Warning and Covenant agents, any CI that replays them.
