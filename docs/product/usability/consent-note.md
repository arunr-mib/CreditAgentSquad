## What this is

- We are testing a prototype lending tool called **Credit Squad**, made by the person who asked you here.
- We are testing the product, not you. If something is hard, that is the product's fault and exactly what we need to see.
- There are no right or wrong answers. Please think aloud: say what you look for, what you expect and what surprises you.

## The data is invented

- **Everything you will see is synthetic.** The bank (Bank of Stoic), every client, person, figure and document is made up for demonstration. Every document is stamped `SYNTHETIC - FOR DEMONSTRATION`.
- **Do not type any real information** into it: no real client, bank, colleague or personal details.
- You will sign in with a shared test account. Its details are not secret, and nothing you type is private.

## What works and what is switched off

- **[agents]** Squad's AI agents are **switched off** on this copy. Everything Squad already did is loaded: its reports, questions, drafts and the cases it raised. You can answer, edit, send, route, return and decide as normal. If you ask Squad a new question, it will tell you that questions are off on this copy. That is expected; we still want to know what you would have asked.
- "Send" on a draft email records it as sent. No real email leaves the system.

## What we write down

- The note-taker writes notes on paper or a laptop. Your name is **not** written in the notes; you are a participant code (P1 to P5).
- **[recording]** We would also like to record the screen and audio. That is a separate yes, asked below, and you can say no.
- If we quote you, the quote is anonymous.
- Notes are used only to find problems in Credit Squad and decide what to fix.

## Your choices

- The session takes about **60 minutes**.
- You can skip any task, take a break, or stop at any time, without giving a reason.
- You can ask us to delete your notes afterwards.
