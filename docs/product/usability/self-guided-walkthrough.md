Owner decision, 23 Nov 2026: no formal usability sessions. The owner walks the hosted copy with this checklist; analysts may try the link on their own and leave an optional note (`feedback-note.md`). The facilitator script, consent note and observation sheet stay in this folder for reference only.

> `SITE` = the hosted v2 address (see `docs/DEPLOY.md`). Password for every account: `squad-demo`.

## Before you start

- A laptop or desktop, browser window at least 1000 px wide.
- Two windows that do not share a sign-in (one normal, one private): one for the analyst, one for the credit officer.
- Times are shown in UTC and say so.
- Agents are off on this copy. Everything Squad already did is loaded, and every person's action works. Asking Squad a new question is refused with a message saying so.
- "Send" on a draft records it. No email leaves the product.
- Steps 3, 6 to 8 and 12 to 14 change the book. Tell the controller when you finish so the book can be reloaded (the ledger is exported first).
